Delete Account & Data

Last updated: August 14, 2026

This page explains, quickly and directly, how to fully delete your Cruising account or remove individual data, without searching through the full Privacy Policy.

Delete your account completely

This can be done directly in the app, and takes under a minute:

  1. Open the app and go to Profile → Settings.
  2. Scroll all the way down to the red "Delete Account" tile.
  3. Tap it and type exactly "DELETE" into the confirmation dialog.
  4. Tap "Delete", your account is gone immediately.

What exactly happens?

Immediately: Your public profile, garage, routes, and friend lists disappear for all other users. Your login access is deleted.

Afterward: Your profile data is moved to a non-public archive, solely for abuse prevention (e.g., evading bans by re-registering) and to clarify legal claims. This archived data is deleted once it is no longer needed for that purpose, at the latest once the statutory limitation periods expire. Details are in section 8 of the Privacy Policy.

Delete individual data only

You don't need to delete your whole account for this, the following data can be removed individually:

  • Vehicles: Profile → Garage → open a vehicle → Delete.
  • Drives / Free Drives: Profile → "Drives" tab → trash icon on the relevant drive.
  • Friends: Friends screen → swipe left or use the "Remove" icon on the relevant person.
  • Profile picture: Profile → camera icon on your profile picture → choose a new picture (replaces/removes the old one).
  • Your own routes: deletable in your own profile under "My Routes".

These deletions take effect immediately and, unlike account deletion, are not subject to archiving.

No access to your account anymore?

If you no longer have the app installed or can't log in anymore, write to us at support@cruising-app.de with the email address tied to your account, and we'll delete your account and data manually.
